Four decades of US tree canopy, county by county

How much of each county is covered by tree canopy, and how has that changed since 1986? Each county's value is the average of millions of 30-metre pixels from USDA Forest Service satellite estimates. Hover or tap a county, search for one, or press play.

    Values are the area-weighted mean of the NLCD Tree Canopy Cover product (v2025.6), so they include water and treeless land in each county. 1986 is the baseline because the 1985 layer has scene gaps. Small changes, under about 1 percentage point, are within the model's noise. Map projection: Albers equal-area; the north arrow is true at the centre of the map, and the scale bar is approximate away from it. Methods, code and full results: github.com/rajibhossain26/us-tree-canopy-change.
